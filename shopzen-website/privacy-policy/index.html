ShopZen Privacy Policy

Welcome to the official Privacy Policy for ShopZen. We believe in complete transparency and data minimization. This document provides clear, audited explanations of how information is handled when you use the ShopZen Android mobile application and visit this website.

Summary of Key Privacy Principles

  • No User Account: ShopZen does not require sign-up, email registration, passwords, or phone numbers.
  • Local-First Storage: Your shopping lists, grocery items, prices, and shopping history are stored 100% locally on your Android device. We operate no cloud databases for your shopping data.
  • No Tracking / Analytics SDKs: ShopZen does NOT contain Firebase, Google Analytics, or third-party crash trackers.
  • Third-Party Integrations: The app utilizes standard Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) with User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent, and Google Play Billing for an optional yearly Remove Ads subscription.
  • Zero Ads on Shopping Screens: Advertisements never appear on Market Mode, Add/Edit Item, Budget screens, or Checkout.

2. Information Handled: Locally Stored Shopping Data

ShopZen is an offline-first utility. The core functionality operates entirely within your Android device's private sandbox storage using Room/SQLite (shopzen.db) and Jetpack DataStore preferences:

  • Shopping Lists: List names, descriptions, target budgets, and assigned ISO 4217 currencies.
  • Shopping Items: Item names, quantities, units of measurement (kg, lb, box, bunch, pcs, etc.), aisle categories, estimated unit prices, actual prices paid, item notes, and purchase status.
  • Shopping History: Immutable snapshot archives created upon trip completion, recording list names, dates, purchased item lists, and total spending.
  • App Preferences: Selected theme (System Default, Botanical Light, Botanical Dark), default currency choice, and local sorting preferences.
Important Guarantee: Barak Websoft does not operate any cloud servers, backend databases, or synchronization APIs for your shopping data. Your lists and prices are NEVER transmitted to Barak Websoft or any remote host.

3. Information Handled by Third-Party SDKs

To maintain transparency, we disclose that ShopZen includes two official, sanctioned Google developer SDKs. These SDKs may transmit technical data directly to Google LLC over secure HTTPS connections:

  • 1. Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob v23.6.0) & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP):
    Used to serve advertisements in non-protected screens and manage regional consent (such as GDPR in the European Economic Area / UK, and CCPA in California). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process device information, IP address, general coarse network connection state, and Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) for ad serving, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and regulatory consent recording.
  • 2. Google Play Billing Library (v7.1.1):
    Used exclusively if you choose to purchase the optional yearly "Remove Ads" subscription. Transactions are processed directly by Google Play Core services. ShopZen does not process, receive, or store your credit card numbers, bank accounts, billing addresses, or payment credentials.
Audited SDK Exclusions: We have audited the ShopZen codebase. The application does NOT integrate Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase, Facebook SDK, Crashlytics, adjust, AppsFlyer, or any third-party behavioral analytics or crash trackers.

4. Advertising and Consent Management

We take a privacy-conscious, user-respecting approach to advertising:

  • Strict Protected Screen Policy: Advertisements are strictly prohibited and technically blocked from displaying on Market Mode, Add/Edit Item, Budget screen, and Checkout surfaces.
  • UMP Regional Consent: For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app presents the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog on first launch. Personalized ads are only requested if you grant consent.
  • Resetting Consent: You can review or reset your advertising privacy choices at any time within the app under Settings > Ad Preferences / Privacy Consent, or by resetting your Advertising ID in Android OS Settings.

5. Subscriptions and Billing Information

ShopZen offers an optional yearly in-app subscription:

  • Entitlement: "Remove Ads" (removes banner advertisements across all app surfaces).
  • Pricing & Terms: Subscription pricing is determined and localized dynamically by Google Play. Terms, renewal dates, and payment processing are managed entirely through your Google Play account.
  • Entitlement Verification: ShopZen verifies active entitlement directly via Google Play Billing cryptographic purchase tokens. We do not store financial secrets on the device or in any remote database.
  • Cancellation & Refunds: Subscriptions can be managed or canceled anytime in the Google Play Store app under Payments & Subscriptions.

6. How Information Is Used

Information handled by ShopZen is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Allowing you to create, organize, edit, and archive grocery shopping lists.
  • Calculating exact running totals, item counts, and budget progress during shopping trips.
  • Delivering tactile one-handed shopping interactions (Market Mode) and haptic feedback.
  • Serving policy-compliant, non-intrusive advertisements on approved surfaces to support ongoing maintenance.
  • Verifying active subscription status via Google Play.

7. Data Storage, Permissions and Security

We enforce strong technical safeguards across the application architecture:

  • Android Sandbox Protection: All local database files (shopzen.db) are stored in Android app-private internal storage, inaccessible to other apps installed on your device.
  • Encrypted in Transit: Any communication between the Google Mobile Ads SDK / Google Play Billing and Google servers is strictly encrypted using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Zero Dangerous Permissions: As verified by our automated security tests, ShopZen requests zero dangerous Android runtime permissions. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, coarse or fine location, SMS, phone state, or external shared storage.
  • Required System Permissions:
    • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for Google Mobile Ads requests and Google Play Billing product catalog checks.
    • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to monitor network availability for consent forms and billing retries.
    • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Declared for Android 13+ advertising ID access by Google Mobile Ads SDK (subject to UMP consent).
    • com.android.vending.BILLING: Included by the Google Play Billing Library to process subscriptions.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your shopping lists reside solely on your device, you have complete control over data retention and permanent deletion:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any individual shopping list or historical snapshot at any time within the app interface.
  • Full Local Wipe: To immediately and permanently erase all shopping lists, history, and preferences, navigate to your Android device settings: Settings > Apps > ShopZen > Storage > Clear Storage (Clear Data).
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling ShopZen immediately purges the local SQLite database and all internal DataStore preference files from your device.
  • No Cloud Deletion Form Needed: Because Barak Websoft stores zero personal shopping data on cloud servers and maintains no user accounts, there is no remote account deletion required.

9. Children's Privacy

ShopZen is a general utility application intended for individuals and families managing grocery shopping. The app is rated Everyone (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone). ShopZen does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). All advertisements served through the Google Mobile Ads SDK adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines. If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party SDKs, please contact us at connect@barakwebsoft.com so we can investigate.

10. User Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Privacy)

Depending on your location, you may have specific statutory privacy rights:

  • Right to Erasure / Access: Your data is stored on your device and can be inspected, exported, or deleted by you at any time.
  • Right to Withdraw Ad Consent (GDPR): Users in the EEA/UK can revoke or alter consent via Settings > Ad Preferences or through device-level advertising settings.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing (CCPA): ShopZen does not sell or share personal data or shopping lists. Third-party ad serving can be restricted via Android's "Delete advertising ID" setting.
