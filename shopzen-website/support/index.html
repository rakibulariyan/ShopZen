Frequently Asked Questions

Click on any question to view verified step-by-step guidance.

From the Home screen, tap the floating green + button in the bottom right corner. Enter a title for your shopping list (such as "Weekly Groceries" or "Holiday Dinner"). You can pick your currency from the ISO 4217 list (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, INR, etc.) and optionally enter a target spending budget. Tap Create List to save.

Open your list and tap the + button at the bottom right. Provide an item name, quantity, unit (kg, lb, box, bunch, pcs, etc.), aisle category, and an optional estimated price. To edit or remove an item later, tap on the item card and select Edit or Delete with confirmation.

Market Mode is tailored for in-store use with one hand. When you enter the supermarket, tap the ⚡ Market Mode button at the top of your active list. Items are presented with large 48dp checkboxes that are easy to tap while pushing a shopping cart. Tapping checks off the item, gives haptic feedback, and updates your live in-cart counter. If you accidentally tap an item, a quick Undo bar appears at the bottom.

ShopZen uses exact integer minor currency arithmetic (avoiding floating-point errors) and half-up rounding. The budget screen displays:

  • Target Budget: The total spending limit you set for the trip.
  • Actual Confirmed Spend: Sum of purchased items with confirmed actual prices.
  • Estimated Total: Sum of estimated costs for all items on your list.
  • Unpriced Warning: If you mark items purchased without entering actual prices, ShopZen explicitly flags them so your budget total remains truthful.

Yes, absolutely. ShopZen is designed as an offline-first application. All your shopping lists, item details, aisle categories, and completed trip archives are stored directly on your phone in an on-device SQLite database. You can use all core shopping features in basement supermarket aisles or remote locations with zero network connection.

Tap the History tab in the bottom navigation bar. When you finish and archive a shopping trip, ShopZen saves an atomic, immutable snapshot. You can open any archived trip to review the date, items purchased, and exact historical total spent, even if the active list is later modified or deleted.

If you change phones or reinstall ShopZen, open Settings and tap Remove Ads or Restore Purchases. ShopZen queries Google Play Billing directly to verify your active yearly entitlement and immediately unlocks your ad-free experience. You will never be charged twice for an active subscription period.

We appreciate bug reports! Please review the Bug Reporting Guidelines below and click the pre-formatted email link to send us details. Including your device model, Android version, and steps to reproduce helps us fix issues quickly.

Because ShopZen does not create user accounts or send your shopping data to any cloud database, you have complete sovereignty over your data right on your device. You can erase any list or clear all data in Android OS Settings (Settings > Apps > ShopZen > Storage > Clear Storage). For privacy inquiries, email connect@barakwebsoft.com or read our Privacy Policy.
