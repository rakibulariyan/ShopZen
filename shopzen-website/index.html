Simple Workflow

How ShopZen Works

Three simple steps to calmer shopping, faster trips, and zero checkout budget surprises.

1

Create your shopping list

Start fresh or duplicate a previous shopping trip in one tap. Give your list a name, choose your currency (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, INR, etc.), and optionally set a spending budget.

2

Add items & organize

Add your groceries with quantities, custom units (kg, lb, box, bunch, pcs), and estimated prices. ShopZen automatically arranges items by aisle category for speedy shopping.

3

Shop, check off & track

Switch to Market Mode at the store. Tap large check targets with one hand as items go in your cart, update real prices, check live spending, and save an immutable history record.

The ShopZen Difference

Why Shoppers Choose ShopZen

We built ShopZen because most shopping list apps are either bloated with mandatory accounts, slow to load, or crammed with intrusive popup ads that interrupt your grocery run.

Strict Zero-Ad Policy on Shopping Screens

Your focus in the supermarket should be on your groceries, not avoiding accidental ad taps. We strictly enforce that advertisements NEVER appear on Market Mode, Add/Edit Item, Budget screen, or Checkout. An optional yearly Remove Ads subscription offers complete ad-free peace of mind across all screens.

100% Private & Local

No account, no sign-up, no email collection. Your shopping items, prices, and notes stay entirely inside your phone's encrypted sandbox storage.

Exact Minor-Unit Math

No binary floating-point math rounding glitches. All financial calculations use integer minor units and rigorous half-up rounding for flawless penny-exact precision.

True Offline Reliability

Never get locked out of your grocery checklist because of basement supermarket dead zones. ShopZen runs with zero network dependency. Open the app, view lists, and check off items anytime, anywhere.
